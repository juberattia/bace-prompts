## CRITICAL RULES FOR ALL PROMPTS

### PRODUCT PLACEMENT — NON-NEGOTIABLE
The BACE hub sits DIRECTLY on the ground surface — cobblestone, asphalt, pavement, sidewalk concrete. **NO concrete foundation. NO pedestal. NO raised platform. NO base slab.** The hub's bottom edge touches the ground surface directly, as if it was simply placed there. This is critical for realism.

### PRODUCT ACCURACY — NON-NEGOTIABLE
The BACE hub is a compact, rectangular container module (approx. 2.3m tall, 3-4m wide) with a GRID OF LOCKER COMPARTMENTS on the front face, a small window section on one side, a touchscreen interface panel, and "bace" branding. The color is WHITE/CREAM (as shown in reference images). Do NOT change its color. Do NOT redesign its form. Reproduce the reference image EXACTLY.

### PEOPLE CASTING — NON-NEGOTIABLE
- Age range: 20-40 years old ONLY
- Gender ratio: 60% women, 40% men
- Ethnicity: DIVERSE — Black, Middle Eastern, South Asian, East Asian, Southern European, Northern European. NEVER all-white. NEVER only blonde women.
- Fashion: COS, Arket, Zara, Uniqlo aesthetic. Earth tones + one color pop per person. NO formal wear.
- Expression: Caught between moments at 70% intensity. NOT peak smile. NOT stock-photo grin.
- Skin: Pores visible on nose and cheeks. Subsurface scattering. NOT airbrushed. NOT plastic.
- Hair: Stray hairs catching light. Random frizz. NOT salon-perfect.
- Teeth: Natural variations. NOT veneer-perfect.

### LIGHTING — NON-NEGOTIABLE
**NO golden hour. NO magic hour. NO amber wash.** These look like stock photography.
Use REAL varied natural daylight instead:
- **Bright overcast**: Soft, even, no harsh shadows — luminous and editorial
- **Direct midday sun**: Hard graphic shadows, high contrast, authentic
- **Open shade**: Subjects in building shade, bright background, natural fill
- **Cloudy-bright**: High white sky, flat soft diffused light, very European
- **Backlit against sky**: Subjects silhouette-edged, bright background
- **Mixed light**: Shop interior warm spill meeting cool natural daylight

Each prompt uses a DIFFERENT lighting condition. This is what makes it look real.

### CAMERA PHILOSOPHY — NON-NEGOTIABLE
Premium cameras with distinctive rendering character. Each prompt uses a different body:
- **Leica M11** + Summilux lenses (35mm f/1.4, 50mm f/1.4) — rangefinder rendering, micro-contrast, 3D pop
- **Hasselblad X2D 100C** + XCD lenses (38mm f/2.5, 55mm f/2.5, 90mm f/2.5) — medium format, tonal richness, shallow plane of focus
- **Leica Q3** (28mm f/1.7 fixed) — wide-angle intimacy, distinctive vignette
- **Fuji GFX 100S** + GF lenses (50mm f/3.5, 80mm f/1.7) — medium format, film-like color science
- **Leica SL2-S** + Summicron 35mm f/2 — clinical sharpness with organic falloff

### ANGLE & PERSPECTIVE — NON-NEGOTIABLE
Every prompt uses a DIFFERENT camera angle. No two prompts share the same perspective:
- Low angle looking up
- Overhead / elevated from balcony
- Through glass reflection
- Dutch tilt / dynamic diagonal
- Straight-on graphic symmetry
- Ground-level ultra-wide
- Through vehicle window
- From behind hub looking outward
- Reflected in wet ground
- High angle from across street
- Long-lens candid compression
- Extreme close-up detail

- **i-D Magazine** — street energy, youth culture, directness

### HOW IT WORKS — THE 4-STEP CUSTOMER FLOW

```
1. APPROACH  → Walk/cycle/drive to your nearest BACE hub
2. SCAN      → Scan the QR code with your phone (NO app needed, NO registration)
3. SELECT    → Choose your service: Pick up / Shop / Drop off / Rent
4. DONE      → Locker opens, take your item or deposit it. Walk away. Under 60 seconds.
```

**Key UX detail for prompts:** People interact with the hub via their PHONE (QR scan) and the hub's TOUCHSCREEN. No keys, no cards, no codes. Phone + screen = the entire interface.

## WAS LÄUFT AUF EINEM BACE HUB — Hub Capabilities & Features

### WHAT IS A BACE HUB?

"Ein bace Hub ist eine modulare Infrastruktur für die letzte Meile."

- For **users:** A place to handle daily errands — pickups, shopping, returns, rentals
- For **cities, real estate, businesses, retailers:** A scalable infrastructure building block
- Controlled via **QR code + web app** — no app download, no registration needed
- Self-developed **hardware + software platform** (bace Plattform)
